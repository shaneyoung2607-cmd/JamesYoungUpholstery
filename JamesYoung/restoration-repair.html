Restoration

Respecting the character of every piece

Restoration is about improving the look and condition of a piece without losing its original charm, value or style. With over 30 years in the trade, we know when to repair, when to replace and when to leave well alone.

We handle both structural repairs, like loose joints, broken frames and worn drawer runners, and cosmetic work such as French polishing, veneer matching and removing heat rings and water marks.

Restoration questions

Can antique furniture be repaired?

Yes. Most antique furniture can be repaired and restored. We assess each piece first and recommend the work that will protect its value and character.

Can you repair a leather sofa?

Yes. We repair tears and cracks, recolour faded or worn leather, and replace panels or fully re-cover in new leather where needed.

Can you remove water marks and heat rings?

Yes, heat ring and water mark removal is part of our restoration service, along with French polishing and veneer repairs.
