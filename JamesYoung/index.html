Services

Upholstery, repairs and restoration under one roof

Every job is quoted free and carried out in our own workshop. No middlemen, no outsourcing.

    Domestic upholstery

FAQs

Upholstery questions, answered

Can't see your question? Call the workshop on 087 204 7560.

How much does it cost to reupholster a sofa?

It depends on the size and style of the sofa, the fabric you choose and how much repair the frame, springs and foam need. Send us a few photos through the quote form and we'll give you a free quote.

Is it worth reupholstering rather than buying new?

For a well-made piece, usually yes. A solid hardwood frame will outlast most new furniture, and reupholstering costs a fraction of a comparable quality replacement while keeping the piece you love.

Can upholstery be repaired?

Absolutely. We can repair the existing fabric, reupholster the piece in new fabric, and repair and restore the wooden parts of the furniture.

Can you repair a leather sofa?

Yes. We repair tears and cracks, recolour faded or worn leather, and replace panels or fully re-cover in new leather where needed.

Do you restore antique furniture?

We do. Our workshop handles antique restoration, French polishing, veneer matching, joint and frame repairs, and traditional upholstery with springs, horsehair and felt.

I need an upholstery shop near me. Can you help?

Our workshop is on Turvey Avenue in North County Dublin and we work with domestic and commercial customers right across the county. If you're further afield, get in touch and we'll talk it through.

Is your work guaranteed?

All upholstery work carries a 12-month guarantee, and our handmade furniture is guaranteed for 10 years.
