Commercial upholstery for Dublin hotels, bars & restaurants

From a single booth repair to reupholstering every seat in the building, we work with hotels, bars, restaurants, theatres and offices across Dublin city centre and the whole county, scheduled around your trading hours.

Built for busy floors

Seating that looks the part and lasts the pace

Commercial seating takes far more wear than anything at home. We strip each piece back to the frame, repair joints, renew springs and foam, and re-cover in fabrics and leathers from trusted suppliers, chosen to stand up to heavy daily use and keep their look.

Clients such as Clontarf Castle Hotel, the Four Seasons, the Gate Theatre, Wesley College and Redwood Design have trusted us with their seating. Whether it is a hotel bar in Dublin 2, a restaurant in Temple Bar, a theatre on Parnell Square or an office in the Docklands, you deal directly with the upholsterers doing the work, with no middlemen.

We can visit your premises anywhere in Dublin to measure up, take samples and give you a clear written quote.

How we work with businesses

No disruption, no surprises

We know a closed bar or an empty restaurant costs you money, so the job is planned around your business.

Site survey anywhere in Dublin

We come to you to measure, assess frames and bring fabric samples. Ideal for city centre premises where taking everything away isn't practical.

Out-of-hours and phased work

We can work early, late or section by section so you stay open while seating is collected, reupholstered and returned.

One-off to full fit-out

Fabric matched to your brand

Collection and delivery

Questions

Commercial upholstery questions

Anything else? Call the workshop on 087 204 7560.

Do you work in Dublin city centre?

Yes. We work with hotels, bars, restaurants, theatres and offices throughout Dublin city centre, including Dublin 1, 2, 4, 7 and 8, as well as the rest of the city and county. We come to you to survey and quote, and collect and return the furniture ourselves.

Can you work outside our opening hours?

Yes. We can plan collections, fitting and on-site work early in the morning, late in the evening or in phases, so your business can stay open.

How big a job can you take on?

Anything from a single chair or booth to reupholstering all the seating in a hotel, bar or theatre. For large jobs we agree a schedule with you in advance.

Do you supply the fabric?

We can source fabric and leather from our trusted suppliers to suit heavy commercial use, or work with fabric specified by you or your designer.

Is commercial work guaranteed?

Yes, all of our commercial upholstery work comes with a 12-month guarantee.
