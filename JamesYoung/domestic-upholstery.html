The workshop

Everything from a single stool to a whole living room

Our upholsterers have been doing this for over 30 years. Every piece is stripped back in our North County Dublin workshop so we can see what needs doing. Frames are repaired and re-glued, springs re-tied or replaced, and new foam, wadding or traditional padding fitted before your new fabric is cut and hand finished.

We collect and deliver right across Dublin, from Swords and Malahide to Rathmines, Ranelagh, Dún Laoghaire and Lucan, or you can call in to our showroom near Dublin Airport to see fabrics and finished pieces.

What we reupholster

  • Sofas, suites and corner units
  • Armchairs, wingbacks and recliners
  • Dining chairs and seat pads
  • Headboards, made to measure or re-covered
  • Window seats, ottomans and footstools
  • Piano stools and cushions
  • Leather sofas and chairs
  • Antique and modern pieces

Done properly

What happens to your piece

Stripped to the frame

Old fabric, padding and tacks are removed so nothing is hidden under the new cover.

Frame and springs repaired

Loose joints are re-glued and re-pinned; broken springs are replaced or hand-tied.

New padding

Fresh foam, wadding, or traditional felt and horsehair, depending on the piece.

Your choice of fabric

Velvet, linen, weaves, boucle or leather. Visit the showroom to see and feel the swatches.

Hand finished

Covers are cut and fitted by hand, with piping, buttoning and studding as required.

Guaranteed

All upholstery work comes with a 12-month guarantee.

How much does it cost to reupholster a sofa?

It depends on the size and style of the sofa, the fabric you choose and how much repair the frame, springs and foam need. Send us a few photos through our quote form and we will give you a free, no-obligation quote.

Is it worth reupholstering rather than buying new?

For a well-made piece, usually yes. A solid hardwood frame will outlast most new furniture, and reupholstering costs a fraction of a comparable quality replacement.

Do you collect and deliver?

Yes, we collect and deliver across Dublin city and county.

Can I see fabrics before deciding?

Yes. Our showroom near Dublin Airport has fabric swatches and finished pieces, and we are happy to advise on what will wear best in your home.
